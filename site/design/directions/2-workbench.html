MCP server for iCloud

Bring iCloud to your AI assistant.

ibridge lets Claude and other AI assistants read and manage your iCloud Mail, Calendar, Reminders and Contacts. You choose read-only or full access, and you can revoke it any time.

What it connects

Four iCloud apps, one connector.

MailRead and manage messages
CalendarRead and manage events
RemindersRead and manage lists
ContactsRead and manage people

Read-only or full access

Read-only lets the assistant look. Full access lets it manage things for you.

  • Read-onlyReads, but cannot send, move, change or delete anything.
  • Full accessReads and manages your mail, events, reminders and contacts.

Encrypted under your key

Your app-specific password is stored encrypted under a key only your connected app holds. Privacy policy

Never your main password

Use an app-specific password. Never use your main Apple ID password.

Revoke any time

Disconnect from your account page whenever you like.

Get started

Connect in four steps

  1. Add the connector

    In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add a custom connector with the address below.

  2. Sign in with Apple

    Claude opens the ibridge sign-in page. Sign in with Apple there.

  3. Choose a plan

    Subscribe for US$1.99/month or US$19.99/year. Tax is added at checkout.

  4. Connect iCloud

    Enter your iCloud address and an app-specific password, never your main Apple ID password. Tick “Read-only access” if the assistant should only read. Revoke it any time.

Questions

Still unsure? The privacy policy and terms spell out the details.

Is ibridge made by Apple?

No. ibridge is an unofficial service. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. iCloud is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Which assistants can use it?

Claude, and other AI assistants that support MCP sign-in. In Claude Code, add it with one command.

Can my assistant change or delete things?

Only with full access. Choose read-only when you connect if you only want it to read. You can revoke access any time from your account page.

Which password do I give it?

An app-specific password, never your main Apple ID password. It is stored encrypted under a key only your connected app holds.

How much does it cost?

The hosted service is US$1.99/month or US$19.99/year, with tax added at checkout. Running it yourself is free.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. ibridge is open source under Apache-2.0. Run it locally in Claude Desktop (.mcpb) or Claude Code, or self-host it with Docker. The code is on GitHub.
