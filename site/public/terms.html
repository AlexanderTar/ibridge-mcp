Terms of use

By signing in to the iBridge service at mcp.ibridge.fyi you agree to these terms. Please read the privacy policy too.

What this is

iBridge is an unofficial service that lets AI assistants use your iCloud Mail, Calendar and Contacts. It is not affiliated with Apple or endorsed by Apple.

Your account and Apple's terms

  • Connect only an iCloud account that is yours, or that you are allowed to use.
  • You are responsible for following Apple's terms for your account and for app-specific passwords.
  • Use only an app-specific password, never your main Apple Account password, and keep your API keys secret. If a key or password may have leaked, revoke it at once, and revoke the app-specific password at Apple.

What you are responsible for

Apps you connect can read your data and send, move, change and delete it. You are responsible for what the apps you connect, and the AI tools behind them, do with your account.

Subscription and payment

The service costs US$1.99/month or US$19.99/year. One subscription covers one iCloud account. You choose monthly or yearly when you subscribe.

  • Billing period and renewal. Each plan runs for one billing period, a month or a year, and renews automatically at the end of it for the same price, until you cancel.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel at any time with Manage billing on the account page. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, and you keep access until then.
  • Payment and tax. Payments are handled by Stripe, which is the seller of record: it charges your card, collects and pays the tax, and handles refunds under its own policy, shown at checkout. To ask for a refund, use the receipt Stripe emails you, or write to taraymovich@gmail.com.
  • A failed payment. If a card payment fails, access continues while Stripe retries it, for up to about two weeks. After that the subscription ends.
  • If you cancel. The tools stop working when your paid period ends. Your connections and API keys are kept, so paying again restores access at once. Deleting your account removes them, and ends your subscription immediately.
