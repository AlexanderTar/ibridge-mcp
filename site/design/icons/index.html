ibridge icon options

Six bridge-and-cloud marks. Each is shown as the bare mark at 16 and 32 px (A, E and F use a simplified variant at 16 px, the same one their favicon uses), the 16 px render magnified ×4 to judge its pixels, the 180 px touch tile, a wordmark lockup and a browser-tab mock, on light and dark. 512 px tiles are further down. Teal, coral and ink are the brand colours; no option uses Apple's white-cloud-on-blue look.
